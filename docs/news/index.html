A Little Love Note

From Pastor Melissa

New year, new beginning. New beginnings are always arriving in each new moment. It’s interesting, though: as we age, we welcome our beginnings with our former wisdom and experiences in mind and some of our old baggage in tow. What might this new year living in the light of God’s love and grace offer?

As we head into 2026, I invite you to draw a star word at our January 4 Epiphany worship service. Or, you can go to God in prayer and ask God for a word. We can pray with our star words and ask God what message God has for us and see how it can guide how we live our lives and open our hearts this year. I also encourage you to prayerfully set aside time to see what comes out when you sit with God’s word for you this year: what comes out of you in the form of art? Is there a story it sparks in you? Or a memory? If you would like a soul friend to companion you on this journey of playfully exploring your star word, please let me know!

My prayer for you in this new beginning is that our God, Alpha and Omega, Beginning and End, is the guiding light that you follow this and every day.

Love,
Pastor Melissa
